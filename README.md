# The World Today — 20 November 2026 in one image

One front page that sums up Friday, 20 November 2026: hours after Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin hail their Shenzhen peace declaration, Russia fires 110 missiles and 400 drones at Ukraine — 8 killed, with Kyiv bridges, the power grid and embassies hit; the International Court of Justice's first-ever genocide verdict finds Myanmar guilty over the Rohingya, and Myanmar calls the ruling "unfair" on Friday as the court orders reparations; Cyclone Akvan makes landfall at Gopalpur at dawn with 3.7 lakh people evacuated and no major loss of life reported; Anthropic slides 8.5% on Friday after its record $81.3bn IPO, the largest in US history; and NASA's Voyager 1 phones home from one light-day away, 16 billion miles from Earth. The ticker along the bottom carries the rest of the day in brief: Modi opens the first SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in New Delhi, France seeks a UN Security Council meeting after the Kyiv strikes, and India A thrash Nepal by 306 runs.

## How it was made

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is composed entirely by `build_image.py` (Python + Pillow) — masthead, flags, Kyiv skyline, scales, cyclone, market chart and Voyager probe are all drawn with shapes, with no external images; re-run `python3 build_image.py` to rebuild it. `main.ts` is the grader entry point: it copies `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
